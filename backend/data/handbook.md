## hours
category: schedule
We are open Monday through Friday, 6:30am to 6:00pm. We are closed on weekends.

## holidays-2026
category: schedule
We are closed New Year's Day, MLK Day, Memorial Day, July 3 (observed), Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day and the Friday after, December 24–25, and December 31. We are open on Veterans Day and Columbus Day.

## tuition
category: billing
Weekly tuition: Infants (6 weeks–12 months) $395; Toddlers (1–3 years) $355; Preschool (3–5 years) $315. Siblings receive 10% off the older child's tuition. Tuition is due on Mondays.

## late-pickup
category: billing
A late fee of $1 per minute applies after 6:00pm and is billed to your account. Three late pickups in one month triggers a meeting with the director.

## illness-policy
category: health
Children must be fever-free (under 100.4°F) for 24 hours without fever-reducing medication before returning. Children are also excluded for vomiting or diarrhea in the last 24 hours, an undiagnosed rash, or pink eye until they have had 24 hours of treatment.

## medication
category: health
Staff administer medication only with a signed authorization form and the medication in its original packaging labeled with the child's name.

## health-assessment
category: health
Pennsylvania requires a current health assessment (CD-51) signed by a physician: every 6 months for children under 3, and every 12 months for ages 3–5. Bring the paper form to the front desk and we validate it on the spot.

## lunch-policy
category: operations
Parents pack lunch. If lunch is forgotten, we provide a backup lunch (sunbutter sandwich, fruit, and milk) for a $6 charge and notify you in the app. We are a nut-free facility.

## allergies
category: health
Every child with an allergy needs an Allergy Action Plan signed by a physician before their first day.

## tours
category: enrollment
Tours are offered Tuesday and Thursday at 10:00am, or by appointment. Book with the front desk or reply here and the director will confirm.

## waitlist
category: enrollment
The infant room typically has a 3–6 month waitlist. A deposit of one week's tuition holds a spot and is applied to your first bill.

## naps
category: operations
Nap and quiet time is 12:30–2:30pm daily. We provide cots; families bring a labeled blanket, which goes home on Fridays for washing.

## what-to-bring
category: operations
Please send two labeled changes of clothes, diapers and wipes if applicable, a water bottle, and seasonal outdoor gear. We go outside daily when it is above 25°F.

## sunscreen
category: health
From April through October, please send a labeled bottle of SPF 30+ sunscreen with a signed authorization form (the same form used for medication). Staff apply sunscreen before outdoor play, and reapply after water activities. We also encourage a labeled sun hat. Children with a sunscreen allergy need a note from a physician.

## vacation
category: billing
Each enrolled child gets one tuition-free vacation week per calendar year. To use it, give at least two weeks' written notice, and the time off must be a full week — Monday through Friday. Partial weeks do not qualify for the credit.

## withdrawal
category: billing
Two weeks' written notice is required for withdrawal or schedule changes. The deposit is forfeited otherwise.

## snow-closures
category: schedule
We follow Wissahocken Area School District weather closures. On delays we open at 9:00am. Notifications go out via the app by 5:45am. There is no tuition credit for weather days.

## family-contact-privacy
category: operations
We never share one family's contact information (phone number, email, or home address) with another family or with anyone outside our staff, under any circumstances. If you need to reach another family — for a playdate or similar — ask the front desk to pass along a message on your behalf.
